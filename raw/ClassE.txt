Class E is mid-level airspace where IFR aircraft are provided with a separation service from other IFR aircraft. Where controller workload permits, IFR aircraft to be given traffic information about known VFR traffic. VFR traffic only receives a flight information service on request. IFR and VFR flights are restricted to 250kts indicated airspeed below 10,000 feet AMSL. In Class E airspace, IFR aircraft must obtain a clearance, but VFR flights do not require a clearance.
